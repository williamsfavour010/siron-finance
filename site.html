YOUR MONEY PLAN

Work through your own numbers

If your income is irregular, use a month you can reasonably count on. If you are between jobs, start with what it costs to get through the next month.

Close the month with the Queens analystOne simulated month, two example workbooks.

In this simplified month, the analyst brought home $5,900. Essentials went $70 over plan and flexible spending went $160 over, including a $35 renewal. With the other contributions unchanged, that left a $230 shortfall. Check what changed before deciding how much to budget next month.

Open the plan and the tracker side by side. Compare the same categories, check the recurring charges, then pick one change for next month. To use a workbook yourself, open it in Google Sheets and use its File menu to download or make a copy if those options are available.

Stress-test a private dealFind the questions an attractive return can hide.

Imagine a friend offers a fixed return on a property project. Start with who owes you the money, what is pledged, and who gets paid before you. Check only what you have independently verified.

Someone you trust can still offer a deal that is too concentrated, illiquid, or hard to enforce. Run a total-loss case and decide what happens to your other goals.
